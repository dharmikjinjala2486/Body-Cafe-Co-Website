## Remove

1. **"The story" essay**: it retells the intro and Why this form.
2. **Key Benefits ring diagram**: it restates the dose and form.
3. **"On the pack" table** (product type, descriptor, claim, diet mark, **barcode**): shoppers don't need it; the claim is on the bottle.
4. **The second cautions list** ("Cautions and interactions").
5. **Duplicate age and suitability lines**: keep one.
6. **The nutrition text line in Ingredients**: the table already shows it.
7. **Manufacturer and licence details, and "Quality and sourcing details"**: owner decision, no manufacturer anywhere.
8. **"What to check on your pack" and the rear-label checklist**: internal label QA, not shopper content.
9. **The pack selector when there is only one pack**: show the pack size as text.
10. **"Frequently bought with" bundle**: duplicates the carousel, and "complete your routine" breaks the compliance guardrail.
11. **The second rating histogram, and stars in the buy box until real reviews exist.**
12. **The Lot report disclaimers repeated twice**: keep one line.
13. **"Preparation"** when it is identical to "Recommended use".

## Keep: the lean product page (same order as Part 1)

1. **Gallery + buy box**: name, one-line key fact, veg / non-veg mark, price + per-serving price, quantity, Add to bag, stock and free-delivery line. Sticky Add to bag on mobile only.
2. **Overview**: headline, a two-sentence intro, "Why this form" in 3 bullets. *Dose and form are stated here once.*
3. **How to take it**: Morning / noon / night (best / also fine), with or without food, the one "keep in mind" line.
4. **Supplement facts**: one table (actives + nutrition), other ingredients, allergens, diet. *Shown once.*
5. **Cautions and storage**: one de-duplicated list, plus age.
6. **Lot record**: one compact form and one line of context.
7. **Reviews**: one summary and the list (marked as sample until real reviews exist).
8. **The rest of the seven**: one row of the other six.

Result: 10 blocks become 8. The dose and form appear in the buy box, the overview and the facts table, and nowhere else.
