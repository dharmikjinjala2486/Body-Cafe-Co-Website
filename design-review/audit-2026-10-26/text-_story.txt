THE STORY IN SHORT

Body Café Co. is built around one idea: the details of a supplement should be easy to find before you choose it, not after. The name comes from the kind of place where asking what's in something is normal. Our first seven products each have their own cap colour, and we're building the records that let you check what we say.

CHAPTER 02

The question we kept asking
#

Most supplement shopping asks for trust first and offers detail later, if at all.

We think it should work the other way round. Before you choose, you should be able to see what's in a product, in what form, and where the record for your bottle lives.

We're making this for curious, sceptical shoppers in India who read labels, compare products and would rather check than be told. You don't need a science degree. You do deserve straight answers, in plain English first, with room for more languages later.

CHAPTER 03

Becoming Body Café Co.
#
Why a café?

A good café is an everyday place. Nobody minds if you ask what's in something, and the menu tells you before you order. We wanted buying supplements to feel the same: unhurried, curious and specific. The café isn't a theme. It's a standard for how a counter should treat a question.

There's no café to visit. The name describes how we want to treat a question, wherever you shop with us.

Playful, never vague

We'd like supplements to feel less clinical and more like something you can talk about. But a light tone is never a reason to blur a fact. If something isn't confirmed yet, we say so plainly instead of filling the gap with a claim.
