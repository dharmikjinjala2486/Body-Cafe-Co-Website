READING THIS INGREDIENT

Vitamin D appears in supplements as D2 or D3, and vitamin K as K1 or K2 forms such as MK-4 and MK-7, according to NIH's Office of Dietary Supplements. Naming the form is part of naming the ingredient.

WHAT TO CHECK ON THE LABEL
Vitamin D3 amount and unit
In mcg or IU. For vitamin D, 1 mcg equals 40 IU, so convert before comparing.
Vitamin K2 form and amount
K2 comes as menaquinones such as MK-4 or MK-7. The label should say which, and how much.
Omega-3 amounts
Omega-3 is included as triglycerides. Look for the amount, ideally with EPA and DHA stated.
Serving and source
Serving size, and the dietary source of each ingredient.
