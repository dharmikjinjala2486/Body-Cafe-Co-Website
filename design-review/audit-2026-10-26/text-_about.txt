CHAPTER 02 · WHY WE EXIST

Why we exist

Most supplement shopping asks for trust first and offers detail later, if at all. We think it should work the other way round.

Before you choose, you should be able to see what's in a product, in what form, and where the record for your bottle lives. You don't need a science degree. You do deserve straight answers, in plain English first.

We'd like supplements to feel less clinical and more like something you can talk about. But a light tone is never a reason to blur a fact. If something isn't confirmed yet, we say so plainly.

CHAPTER 03 · THE NAME

Why “Body Café Co.”?

A good café is an everyday place. Nobody minds if you ask what's in something, and the menu tells you before you order.

That's the counter we want to run: unhurried, curious and specific. The café isn't a theme. It's a standard for how a shop should treat a question.

There's no café to visit. The name describes how we want to treat your questions, wherever you shop with us.
