NO. 7

Understand this report
Lot (batch)
Units made together under the same conditions. A report describes one lot, not the product forever.
Sample
The portion of a lot (or of an ingredient) that was actually tested. Results concern that sample and the tests listed.
Reported result
Shown exactly as the laboratory wrote it, with its unit. “<0.05” means below 0.05, the lowest level that test could report, not zero.
Not detected (ND) and LOD / LOQ
“Not detected” means below the test's detection limit (LOD). Results below the quantification limit (LOQ) are often written with “<”.
Specification
The stated limit a result is read against, with where that limit comes from. Without one, a result can't be called a pass.
Assessment
Whether a result meets its stated specification. Shown only when the report or our approved review states the specification and its source.
Method
The named procedure used. Different methods can detect different levels.
Laboratory accreditation
An independent body's recognition of a laboratory's competence for specific tests (its scope). It applies to the laboratory and those tests, not to Body Café Co. or our products.
Version and superseded
If a report is corrected or replaced, the new version is published and the older one stays listed as superseded, with the reason.
What a report can’t tell you
A report describes a sample from one lot and the tests listed. It doesn't cover tests that weren't done.
It doesn't authenticate an individual pack. A lot number can be copied; no QR code or lookup on this page proves a bottle is genuine.
It isn't a diagnosis, a safety guarantee for every person, or a promise of results.
