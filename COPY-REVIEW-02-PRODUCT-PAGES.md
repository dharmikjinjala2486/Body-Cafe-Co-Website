# Copy review 02: Content Focus product pages, every line

**Status:** applied to Content Focus, 20 Nov 2026 (owner: "Yes, apply those"), except the ⚠️ rows and the trust strip, which wait for the facts in section 5. In the batch block, "One report per production batch." was dropped rather than replaced with the lab names; the heading is "Tested, batch by batch." and the line under it "Type the Batch No. from your bottle to see its lab report." The Questions section has three answers per product (who, how, vegetarian) and Iron a fourth; "What is tested in each batch?" waits for the lab report contents.
**Covers:** every sentence on the seven Content Focus product pages (`?design=e&part=5`), read from the local preview, hidden tabs included.
**Compared with:** Sports Research, Thorne, Ritual, Momentous and The Whole Truth (product and home pages, read 20 Nov 2026).
**Left alone on purpose:** the label's own words (directions sentence, allergens, cautions, storage, supplement facts, other ingredients) and the front-label claim ("Supports heart, brain & joint health."). These have to match the pack.

Marks: **Keep** = already good · ⚠️ = needs a fact confirmed first · **W** = a Version E workbook line (owner said "match all"), so changing it overrides that.

## 1. What the five brands do better

| # | Pattern | Example from their pages | Where ours falls short |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every fact ends in a "so you…" | Sports Research: one mini-gel "so you have more time to play" | Ours stops at the fact: "Triglycerides, as found in fish." |
| 2 | They say what they chose instead | Ritual: methylated B12 "as opposed to the commonly used cyanocobalamin" | We name the form but never say why it matters to the buyer |
| 3 | They talk about taking it | Sports Research: "No fishy burps", "easy to swallow" | Only our sample reviews do this; our own copy never does |
| 4 | Proof with a consequence | Momentous: if a run fails, "it doesn't hit your shelf"; Thorne: four rounds of testing on every batch | Our batch block says "One report per production batch." and never says who tests it or what is tested |
| 5 | No jargon left unexplained | Thorne explains ethyl ester as "more concentrated… with fewer capsules" | "Ferrochel® chelate, neutrally charged", "cholecalciferol", "Free-form", "36:1–44:1" |
| 6 | Each section answers one buyer question | Thorne and Ritual: "Is it right for me?", "What testing do you complete?" | We have no product FAQ at all |
| 7 | Local, human voice | The Whole Truth: "#nothingtohide", "Bada Barkhana" | "Iron first. Chai later." is the one line of ours that does this |

Our copy is about **our label**: "printed", "on the front" and "named" come up 9 times in the seven descriptions, and "so you" only twice. The fix is not more words. It is turning each fact into what the buyer gets.

**Not to copy:** "leads the industry", "superior", "the best" (house rule 1); Thorne's nine-benefit lists (we may only use the front-label claim); celebrity and athlete proof; any promise of how soon it works.

## 4. New: Questions (3 per product, label facts only)

The same three questions on every page, as Thorne and Ritual do. Answers come only from the label and the batch records. They give no medical advice and say nothing about how soon it works.

1. **Who is it for?** "Adults. If you’re pregnant, breastfeeding or under medical care, ask your doctor first." (label caution)
2. **How do I take it?** The label's directions in one line, e.g. Iron: "One capsule each morning on an empty stomach. No milk or chai for 2 hours before or after."
3. **What is tested in each batch?** ⚠️ needs the list of tests from the lab reports (e.g. the label amount, heavy metals, microbes).

Iron, written out:
- *Who is it for?* Adults. If you’re pregnant, breastfeeding or under medical care, ask your doctor first.
- *How do I take it?* One capsule each morning on an empty stomach. No milk or chai for 2 hours before or after.
- *Can I take it with my other supplements?* Take it on its own. Calcium can lower how much iron you absorb.
