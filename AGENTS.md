Online payment (13 Nov 2026) is Razorpay Standard Checkout, on the shared payment page (`/payment/:id`, drawn by `web/part2-e.js` for Parts 2, 3, 4 and Content Focus). Pay asks `POST /api/orders/:id/pay` for a Razorpay order (its id is recorded on our order as `rzp_order` so Pay reuses it; the receipt, our order number, is the fallback search, which Razorpay updates about half a minute late), opens Razorpay's window, and sends its signed answer to `POST /api/orders/:id/pay/verify`; only the API marks an order paid, after checking the signature and reading the Razorpay order back (receipt, amount, a successful payment). `app/razorpay.py` in the local preview, `supabase/functions/operations/razorpay.js` plus the pay routes in `core.js` on the live site; keep the two in step. The keys are `RAZORPAY_KEY_ID` and `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET` (git-ignored `.env` locally, function secrets when hosted); test keys (`rzp_test_…`) take no money, and the secret never goes in the code or the browser. Pinned comments (18 Nov 2026, owner: "like PDF highlights, like Figma comments") are part of tester feedback, in the same two files. The panel's "Comment on the page" turns on comment mode (`body.tf-commenting`): a click anywhere on the page, or on text highlighted first, drops a pin with a box for the note, and does nothing else (the click is caught on `window` in the capture phase). The note is an ordinary tester note with a `pin` in its data: `sel` (the element's address: nearest ancestor id, then tag and `nth-of-type` at each step), `x` and `y` (0 to 1 inside that element), `text` (the highlighted words, or the element's first words) and `hl` (the tester highlighted them). A pin is never a screen position, so it lands in the same place on a phone and a desktop; if the selector no longer holds those words the pin moves to the smallest element under `#main` that does, and if neither is found the note stays in the lists with its quote. Every tester sees the numbered pins of the page's unresolved notes for the design they are in (Part 2 and Content Focus keep separate pins; `GET /api/tester-feedback` now also returns `design` and `pin`); clicking one shows the note and its status, and "Show comments on the page" in the panel hides them on that device. A pinned note's "Show on the page" in the table opens its pin. Pin limits, the same on both servers: `sel` up to 400 characters of letters, digits, spaces and `#>:()_-`, `text` up to 200, and one of the two must be present.
