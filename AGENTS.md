# Storefront design versions

Variant E Part 1 is the approved design. A, B, C, D, F, G and H are archived. Preserve their presentation when working on new design revisions.

Variant E Part 2 (`?design=e&part=2`, "Creative") and Part 3 (`?design=e&part=3`, "Minimal") are complete. Preserve their presentation.

Variant E "Part 2 · Content Focus" (`?design=e&part=5`) is a working duplicate of Part 2 for content changes. It runs as Part 2 (`body[data-variant-e-part="2"]`, same files, data and connected store) and adds `body[data-content-focus="1"]`; scope anything specific to it under that attribute so Part 2 stays as it is. Its new copy (from the client's Version E Copy Transformation workbook) lives in `web/content-focus-e.js` and `web/content-focus-e.css`.

Part 2's inner pages (Our standard, Learn index, Story, Help index, Look up a batch) and its finishing touches on the bag, checkout and sign-in live in `web/part2-pages-e.js` + `web/part2-pages-e.css` (12 Nov 2026), scoped to `body[data-variant-e-part="2"]` with the `p2p-` class prefix (`pp-` belongs to the older product page). They replace the shared pages for Part 2 and Content Focus only; Parts 3 and 4 keep the versions in `web/part2-e.js`. The same files dress Contact, the five policy pages and Preferences for Part 2 (class `p2p-su`): those keep the shared markup and behaviour from `web/support.js` and gain a policy switcher, a scroll-tracking contents card and Part 2 styling. Their sentences are kept whole because `web/content-focus-e.js` swaps copy by exact text and reads `.p2-std-status`, `.p2-rules` and `.p2-actions-left`.

Apply new design revisions only to Variant E Part 4 (`?design=e&part=4`, "The Café"): the brand is a café that serves seven things and says exactly what is in each. Its own chrome, nothing visual shared with Parts 1–3: Anton display type, the seven cap colours as full-bleed blocks, the bottle stills and cap close-ups (`web/media/premium/`) filling the screen, menu lines with dot leaders. The mechanic is Service (Morning · Noon · Night): a header switch, stored in `sessionStorage` and applied as `body[data-p4-service]`, that changes the palette (paper / white / ink) and, from each label's `label_timing`, which menu items stand forward; product pages open in the service their label says is best. Café words are limited to: Now serving, Service, Menu, House rules, Regulars, House notes, Order number, Opening hours, Not on the menu. Nothing may suggest taking all seven. Same content, flow, data and backend as Parts 2 and 3. Part 4 lives in `web/part4-e.js` + `web/part4-e.css` (scoped to `body[data-variant-e-part="4"]`) and uses the shared Part 2 layer (`web/part2-e.js`, `web/part2-e.css`, which serve Parts 2, 3 and 4) for data helpers, bottles, spins and the add / lot-picker / filter / frequently-bought-with behaviours; a change to the shared files must leave Parts 2 and 3 looking the same. Keep Part 1 as the production design unless the user explicitly requests a promotion. No label photographs anywhere in E (the rear labels print the manufacturer).

The store assistant (12 Nov 2026) is an AI chat for Part 2 and Content Focus only: `web/chat-e.js` + `web/chat-e.css` (prefix `p2c-`, scoped to `body[data-variant-e-part="2"]`). The assistant is called Barista; its character is the cream bottle in an indigo cap and apron (`web/media/barista/`, drawn for the site, no label on the body). It posts the conversation to `POST /api/chat`, which calls Claude (`claude-opus-5-5`) and streams the answer back as plain text: `supabase/functions/operations/chat.js` on the live site, `app/chat.py` in the local preview (it reads its rules, limits and knowledge from the hosted files, so edit those). What the assistant knows about the shop is the live catalogue plus `chat-knowledge.js`, generated from `web/support-content.js`; after changing that file run `node deploy/build_chat_knowledge.mjs`. The API key is the `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` secret (function secret when hosted, environment variable locally) and never goes in the code or the browser. Its rules keep the site's own: store facts only from our content, no personal medical advice, never suggest all seven, never name the manufacturer.
